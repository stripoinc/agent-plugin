# Role-tagging contract

Read this only when the packet lists `unresolvedRoles` you intend to act on.
The contract's rule — never author a deterministic role hint — is the whole
of what you need on a clean run.

## Role-tagging contract

**READ THIS FIRST: none of the rules below is enforced by a refusal any more.**
Every role-coverage finding — an authored hint, an unmeasured value, a missing
role — is now RECORDED at `severity: "gap"` and the run publishes the kit
anyway. That is deliberate. A refused run writes nothing, so under replace-only
persistence the account keeps the previous site's brand or keeps nothing at all,
and neither is better than a kit that says which of its styling values is soft.

Three consequences you have to hold together:

1. **`severity` no longer tells you anything on this axis.** An honest gap, an
   authored hint and an unmeasured value all read `"gap"`. `reason` is the
   discriminator: `no_evidence` (a deterministic-lane miss; Phase 2.5 may act when context identifies a target),
   `authored_without_evidence`, `value_not_measured`, `role_not_produced`.
2. **These prohibitions are now the whole protection, not a prediction.** They
   used to be backed by a gate that stopped the run; the gate still detects
   every violation and still names it, and then the value ships. Obeying the
   contract is what keeps a fabricated font or colour out of a customer account.
3. **What still stops a run is a short list and none of it is styling:** a
   corrupt schema, an unsafe public string, a wrong-site capture, a hand-edited
   diagnostics file, an unreadable gate input. Contacts, social URLs and asset
   URLs are the factual family and keep every gate they had.

Role hints in `usageHints` arrays describe how a record is used downstream. Outside the bounded candidate and run-bound recovery paths below, the agent appends hints only to existing scaffolder records that match the evidence and never creates a new record to carry a hint. Normalize may create one carrier from validated `agent-role-recovery.json`; this is a machine-checked recovery result, not an authored-role shortcut.

**The deterministic roles are the exception, with only machine-bounded carve-outs.** The agent may NEVER author on its own reading — on any row, on any evidence, at any confidence: the three productCard mirrors (`product-name-typography`, `product-price-typography`, `product-old-price-typography`), the five always-required typography roles (`heading-`, `body-`, `header-`, `footer-`, `button-typography`), or the text-colour region roles, which are exactly `heading-text` and `body-text`. These roles are produced deterministically, copied from the selector-free candidate under the next paragraph's equality rule, or filled through Phase 2.5's run-bound recovery artifact. A DOM re-probe must be copied exactly; a screenshot may supply only a color after eligible re-probe failure; typography fallback is a complete captured tuple or the fixed system tuple and is labelled `defaulted`. The prohibition does NOT cover `header-link`, `footer-link` or `link-text`, which the agent owns and may tag from evidence. A hand-added FORBIDDEN ROLE asserts styling evidence that none of these machine-bounded paths produced; it ships only as an unsupported, provenance-labelled value. When a FORBIDDEN ROLE is missing after both bounded recovery lanes are exhausted, leave it as an explicit gap and continue rather than inventing a value or blocking the Brand Kit.

**The selector-free candidate exception is bounded by a value, not a judgement.** When — and only when — the run records a region role at `severity: "gap"` (`typographyRoleCoverage` or `textColorRoleCoverage`), you may tag that role onto a row, or create ONE row to carry it, on a single condition: **the row's value must equal a candidate this run published for that role from the selector-free capture** — an entry in `assembly-diagnostics.json.candidates["<role>"]` carrying `"source": "salient-text"`. Copy it verbatim — for typography the `(family, weight, sizePx)` triple, for a `textColors` row the hex — and create the row already carrying the hint, never empty. You may not adjust, round, average or eyeball a value, and an entry without the `salient-text` marker licenses nothing. `role_not_produced`, or a role with no salient candidate, is unfillable through THIS candidate-copy lane only; use Phase 2.5 when the current screenshot/DOM identifies a concrete semantic target. Likewise, the salient lane emits no candidates for `header-typography`, `footer-typography`, or `button-typography`, but Phase 2.5 may exact-reprobe and, after eligible failure, supply a labelled complete typography fallback. If neither lane has a valid target/source, leave the gap and continue. These salient candidates are capped at `0.75`: they stand on one leg of evidence (measured on the page; no selector vouching that it IS the role), so confirm the row against the screenshot before you copy it. For `heading-typography` only, normalize now performs this copy itself — where no row carries the role, no selector produced it, and `candidates["heading-typography"]` holds exactly one `"source": "salient-text"` entry at `0.75`, the pass appends that row verbatim and it is published `defaulted`, so there is nothing left for you to copy on those runs.

**The other half of the same rule: the VALUE on a row that already carries a region role is checked too.** The exception above covers a role the page could not supply. Where the page CAN supply it — the capture holds an `h1`/`h2`/`h3`, a `<header>`, a `<footer>` — the role is still produced deterministically, and the value on the row must be one this capture measured for that role. `textColorRoleCoverage` refuses any `heading-text` / `body-text` hex that is not a colour this capture measured on a row that role derives from — the `h1`..`h6` rows of `text-styles.json` for `heading-text`, its `p` rows for `body-text`, after the same usability filter the synthesiser applies. The colour `text-color-role-synthesis.js` derives is always one of those, so it always passes; a colour on no such row does not, whether it was picked off a screenshot or nudged after the fact. `typographyRoleCoverage` refuses a row carrying one of the five typography region roles on **every** value field, not just the signature: `(family, weight, sizePx)` must be the signature of a captured row this role's selector mapper selects; the `family` **string** must additionally be spelled by some captured row carrying that signature — the string check exists because the signature match compares only the first font name in the list and every declared fallback after it would otherwise go unmeasured. `product-card-styles.json` does **not** license a family here: that arm existed and was removed, because a card's spelling reaches a region-role record only by winning the canonical-family merge, whose winner is chosen from `brand.typography[]` peers the gate does not hold and must not read off the artifact it is judging. And `lineHeightPx`, `fontStyle`, `letterSpacingPx` and `textTransform` must each be the value some captured row *with that same signature* carries. The split is not a judgement — it is what the two dedup passes can establish: `dedupeTypographyComponents` groups on `(family, weight, sizePx)` alone and unions every peer's `usageHints` onto the first peer's other fields, so a role hint legitimately travels onto a record whose line-height came from a sibling row. Both channels record `reason: "value_not_measured"`, and the run does NOT stop: the row is published carrying a value the capture does not support, and the finalize report says so. Whether the account gets the real value therefore depends on you applying the repair below. **Which repair is right turns on one fact, and the entry hands it to you — you are never asked to work it out.** A `typographyRoleCoverage` entry carries an `anchoredCarrier` field: a string naming a DIFFERENT row that carries the same role and IS anchored to the capture, or `null` when no row carrying the role is anchored. `textColorRoleCoverage` does not emit the field at all, so a colour refusal is always the `null` case.

**Where `anchoredCarrier` is `null` — and always on the colour channel — removing the hint does not fix it and makes it worse.** The role is derivable here and nothing carrying it is anchored, so stripping the hint turns a bad value into a MISSING role — `reason: "role_not_produced"`, a producer fault — rather than the honest gap it might look like; and hints are never stripped by hand ([Hint preservation](#role-tagging-contract) below). Set the row back to a measured value: for a typography REGION role, one captured row for the role copied verbatim out of `text-styles.json` — copying a single row satisfies both scopes at once, which is why that repair always works; for a productCard MIRROR role, the `family` / `weight` / `sizePx` / `lineHeightPx` of the `productCard[0]` field the entry names, because that channel is not produced from a probe selector and `text-styles.json` holds no value for it at all; for a colour, the hex `text-color-role-synthesis.js` derives, which the entry names for you in its message and in `derivedValue`.

**Where `anchoredCarrier` NAMES a row, the repair is the opposite one, and it is a sanctioned removal.** Remove the role hint from the unanchored row the entry refused, and leave the named record exactly as it is. The role stays present and covered by that anchored record, so nothing goes missing — whereas copying a captured row onto the anchored record would overwrite a genuinely measured value to satisfy a gate that is pointing at a different row. The entry's own message says so in those words (`THE REPAIR IS TO REMOVE THE STRAY HINT FROM THE UNANCHORED ROW`) and names the record you must not touch. This is the second and last removal a gate ever asks for; see [Hint preservation](#role-tagging-contract) below.

**Correcting a synthesised COLOUR against the screenshot is allowed, and the reason must name what it corrects.** Prefer Phase 2.5: exact re-probe first, then a hash-bound in-image color sample only when that probe fails with an eligible code. The legacy justification escape remains for a `heading-text` / `body-text` hex that this capture measured on none of the role's rows: `assembly-diagnostics.json.roleChoiceJustifications["brand.colors.textColors"]` must name the role hint, the hex kept, and the helper-derived hex overridden. Write it before normalize. It accepts the value but does not turn it into a measurement: normalize grades it `defaulted` and finalize warns. A silent deviation is recorded unsupported. The exception expires when any member of that tuple changes and never applies to typography; fonts are not inferred from screenshots.

This rule used to live in the trailing sentence of a bullet about something else, while the two paragraphs above it read as a general grant. Runs split on which they obeyed. A production run on a site with no headings followed the grant and tagged the PRODUCT PRICE row as the brand's heading typography, because the gate's own error text named the row matching `productCard[0]` and on that site the price row is the only one. The gate text has been fixed too; this paragraph exists so the contract does not depend on it.

- **Hint preservation.** Existing hints on a scaffolder-produced or normalize-injected record are never stripped. When you edit a record (e.g. to update its `description`), the `usageHints` array stays as-is or grows; it does not shrink. The only place hints may be removed is inside a normalize safety-net helper (`tagProductCardCtaMirrors` strips `product-card-cta` from buttons whose description mismatches; `applyHeaderLinkStrip` strips `header-link` / `footer-link` when scoped probe evidence proves the colour doesn't appear in that region). Agent-side stripping has been the root cause of past regressions where a brand-primary accent colour or a scoped header-link colour silently disappeared from the role-tagged output even though the underlying probe evidence still supported it.
  - **The two removals a gate asks you for, and their bounds.** Exactly two entries instruct you to remove a hint. Both name the row, neither is a judgement you make, and in both the entry itself is the authorisation — this is not "stripping a hint the evidence supports", it is the opposite case.
    1. **`reason: "authored_without_evidence"`.** The page offers no evidence for the role, nothing could have produced it there, so the hint asserts a page fact; removing it leaves an honest gap the kit persists with.
    2. **`reason: "value_not_measured"` on a `typographyRoleCoverage` entry whose `anchoredCarrier` names another row.** A second record already carries the role and IS anchored to the capture, so removing the stray hint from the refused row leaves the role present and covered. Remove it from that row only, and leave the named record exactly as it is — copying a captured row over it would destroy a measured value to satisfy a gate pointing elsewhere.

    **`reason: "value_not_measured"` with `anchoredCarrier: null` is neither of them** and its remediation is never removal: the role IS derivable there and nothing carrying it is anchored, so an absent hint is a missing role (`reason: "role_not_produced"`), not an honest gap, and the repair is to put the measured value back ([the VALUE on a row that already carries a region role](#role-tagging-contract) above). `textColorRoleCoverage` emits no `anchoredCarrier`, so a colour `value_not_measured` is always that case and removal is never its repair. The field and the message are built from one computation in the gate, and the repair prose `--mode normalize` records reads that same field rather than recomputing it, so the three cannot disagree; if they ever appear to, report it and stop rather than choosing between them -- that is an internal contradiction in the run's own output, not a styling grade, and it is the one case in this section where stopping is right.

- **Typography mirrors.** The scaffolder pre-tags `product-name-typography`, `product-price-typography`, and `product-old-price-typography` on the `brand.typography[]` record whose `(family, weight, sizePx)` matches the corresponding `productCard[0]` mirror (`titleTypography` / `priceTypography` / `oldPriceTypography`, with ±1px size tolerance). When no record matches the probe signature, the scaffolder synthesises a new `brand.typography[]` entry from the productCard mirror evidence (mechanics: [normalize-safety-nets.md](references/normalize-safety-nets.md), step 8 — Typography mirrors) so the role hint lands without manual authoring. You MUST NOT add these tags directly, and you may remove one only in the single case the gate itself authorises: a `value_not_measured` entry whose `anchoredCarrier` names a DIFFERENT, anchored carrier of the same role (see [the two removals a gate asks you for](#role-tagging-contract) above). If a role remains missing, use Phase 2.5 only when the screenshot/page context identifies a semantic target: exact re-probe first, then complete captured/system typography fallback if that probe fails. Normalize creates the carrier from validated recovery evidence and labels a fallback `defaulted`; otherwise report the gap and continue.
  - **`reason: "no_evidence"` is an honest deterministic gap, not a blocker.** It means the selector mapper could not derive the role, not that the screenshot/page context visibly lacks one. If that context identifies a concrete semantic element, Phase 2.5 is the one bounded recovery: exact DOM re-probe first, with screenshot-color or complete captured/system typography fallback only after eligible failure. If no concrete target exists, leave the gap and continue. `reason: "role_not_produced"` is handled the same way for persistence: bounded recovery when there is a real target, otherwise report it and continue; never type an unsupported value directly into a carrier.
  - **Duplicate-hint advisory.** If `typographyRoleCoverage` contains a `severity: "info"` entry about a duplicate productCard mirror hint, **do nothing**. The deterministic helper resolves this on the next normalize pass. Do NOT edit `usageHints` on typography rows to "fix" it — that is the forbidden author/strip path. If the entry persists across a clean re-run of `--mode normalize`, file a BACKLOG item naming the typography signatures involved; the fix is upstream in the scaffolder's dedup/merge passes, not in the agent's edits.

- **`product-card-cta` tagging — reusable CTA evidence.** Normalize's canonical trust gate requires `productCard[0].evidenceQuality` to be `medium` or `strong`, a non-empty `cta.text` after ECMAScript `String.trim`, `cta.textSource: "visible-text"`, `cta.hasUsableVisibleText: true`, and `cta.isIconLike` not true. `layoutIntent` records geometry; it neither grants nor revokes trust. Only after that gate passes does `tagProductCardCtaMirrors` deterministically find the existing `button[]` / `typography[]` entry whose visual signature (background, font, border, radius / family, weight, sizePx) matches `productCard.cta` and append `product-card-cta` to its `usageHints`. You MUST NOT add this tag, and may remove it only under the same gate-authorised exception as the typography mirrors above. Verify the mirror exists on exactly one entry after `--mode normalize`; if missing for canonically reusable evidence, file a BACKLOG item naming the visual signature involved (the fix is upstream in the helper, not in agent edits).

- **`product-card-cta` exclusivity — nonreusable CTA evidence.** When the canonical gate above fails — weak/missing quality, blank/missing/non-visible text, icon-like action, or a missing CTA — normalize ensures no entry in `button[]`, `typography[]`, or `brand.colors.{accentColors, backgroundColors, textColors}` carries `product-card-cta` or any `product-card-cta-*` color hint. Phase 2.5 recovery is inside the same boundary: it must not fill, retain ownership of, or grade `product-card-cta`, `product-card-cta-background`, `product-card-cta-text`, `product-card-cta-hover-background`, or `product-card-cta-border` unless `productCard[0]` passes that gate. Verify by reading the post-normalize output; if any such entry remains tagged, again file a BACKLOG item — do not strip the hint by hand.

- **Color usageHint channel constraints.** Each color usageHint belongs to one canonical channel. Co-tagging is permitted only in the cases listed below. The customise compiler uses channel as a semantic signal — placing a `textColors`-meaning hint on an `accentColors` row causes the compiler to skip the channel-scoped lookup and fall back to channel-agnostic resolution, which mis-paints H1–H6 in a red sale color when the row visually serves "promotional emphasis."

  Channel mapping (treat as a hard rule; override only on screenshot evidence the channel-preferred row is wrong, not stylistic preference):

  - **backgroundColors[] only** — `canvas-background`, `content-background`, `header-background`, `footer-background`, `product-card-surface-background`, `promo-surface-background`.
  - **accentColors[] only** — `brand-primary-accent`, `brand-secondary-accent`, `promo-accent`, `promo-surface-accent`, `button-primary-background`, `button-primary-hover-background`, `button-secondary-background`, `product-card-cta-background`, `product-card-cta-hover-background`, `product-card-cta-border`, `divider`, `border-subtle`.
  - **textColors[] only** — `heading-text`, `body-text`, `link`, `header-link`, `footer-text`, `footer-link`, `button-primary-text`, `button-secondary-text`, `product-card-cta-text`.
  - **Dual-channel (legitimate)** — `link-text`, `price-current`, `price-old`. These describe the same visible colour from two angles: as a brand-emphasis accent (accentColors row) AND as a text rendered in that colour (textColors row). When the brand uses one hex for both roles, emit a SINGLE record per channel, each carrying ONLY that channel's hints — do NOT merge the two semantic roles into one row.

  Counter-examples (forbidden):
  - Do NOT add `heading-text` or `body-text` to an `accentColors[]` row, even when the row's description prose mentions "promotional headings," "title color," or "headline emphasis." If a sale color is used in promo headlines, that is a typography/promo-surface concern, NOT a `heading-text` tag — `heading-text` is the reader-facing copy color the compiler resolves for the email's H1–H6 tokens. A wrong tag here repaints every email heading in the accent color.
  - Do NOT add `product-card-cta-text` to an `accentColors[]` row. CTA text is a text role; emit a separate `textColors[]` row carrying `product-card-cta-text`. The accent row that holds the CTA outline / border colour carries `product-card-cta-border` instead.
  - Do NOT add `promo-surface-background` (or any other `*-background` hint) to an `accentColors[]` row. Surface hints describe filled regions and live on `backgroundColors[]`.

- **Dedup.** Two records sharing a visual signature are the same evidence. Typography signature: `family` + `weight` + `sizePx`. Button signature: `backgroundColor` + `fontColor` + `borderColor` + `borderRadius`. ProductCard signature: `priceColor` + `cta-shape` + `cta-text` + `surface.backgroundColor`. Sample-volatile fields (`oldPriceColor`, top-level border, `surface.borderColor` / `borderRadius`) are intentionally excluded from the key — they are restored on `productCard[0]` post-dedup by propagation passes. Keep one record per signature; merge their `usageHints`. Avoid arrays where many entries share an identical signature. Buttons whose `backgroundColor` is empty AND have no outline (`borderColor`/`borderWidth`) AND no role-naming hint are dropped post-dedup by `lib/button-noise-filter.js`. The same helper collapses rows sharing the relaxed signature `(backgroundColor, fontColor, borderColor, borderRadius)` — padding is intentionally not part of the relaxed key. Authoring multiple records that differ only in padding is unnecessary; the helper merges them and unions their `usageHints`.

- **Cross-channel hint violations are a normalize-time concern.** When `--mode normalize` detects a `heading-text` or `body-text` hint on a non-canonical channel record (specifically, on an `accentColors[]` row), `lib/text-role-hints-strip.js` strips the offending hint and emits an info-severity entry in `assembly-diagnostics.json` at `$.brand.colors.accentColors[i].usageHints`. The agent does not author `heading-text` or `body-text` at all (see the deterministic-roles paragraph above), so on those two hints this helper should now have nothing to strip; an entry here means something upstream put a hint where it does not belong. The rule still applies to hints the agent DOES own: put them on the canonical channel rather than relying on the strip. `link-text` is intentionally NOT stripped (brand-coloured links are legitimate; the customise compiler's `link` resolver matches this).

`--mode normalize` runs deterministic safety nets (dedup, role-mirror tagging, icon-only demotion, old-price + button-dimension backfill, description-mismatch strip, header/footer-link strip, non-ecom drop). They absorb run-to-run variance, not substitute for the contract above. Expect agent edits to be mutated in these specific shapes after normalize. Full helper list and run order: [normalize-safety-nets.md](references/normalize-safety-nets.md).
